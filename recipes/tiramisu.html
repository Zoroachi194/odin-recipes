Tiramisu

Description

This is my favourite Italian desert, because it is so easy to make at home, and it takes very little ingredients.

It is probably the most famous Italian desert and it becomes done in under an hour. The resting in the fridge is what makes it a longer recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 package of Ladyfingers
  • 500g of Mascarpone
  • 5 Eggs
  • 150g of Sugar
  • 50g of Espresso
  • 80g of Cocoa powder

Steps

  1. Mix the espresso, with two cups of hot water and put in the fridge to cool, while making the cream
  2. Divide the egg whites and yolkes into two different bowls.
  3. Into the bowl with the egg yolkes, put half of the sugar and mix until it dissolves fully. Then add the mascarpone little by little, while mixing well, until you reach a smooth consistency.
  4. Mix the egg whites into stiff peaks, and then very slowly add the remaining sugar little by little, while continuing to mix (preferably with a hand mixer).
  5. Incorporate the egg whites mixture, little by little, in the bowl with the mascarpone and the egg yolkes, mixing with a spatula, trying to keep as much air as possible in the mixture.
  6. Take out the espresso and dip each ladyfinger in for about a second and a half, before making the first layer of the tiramisu with them.
  7. Make an even layer on top of the ladyfingers with the cream. Repeat this process until you are out of ladyfingers and cream.
  8. Dust cocoa powder trough a sieve on top of the tiramisu. Cover with a lid or saran wrap, and put in the fridge overnight. You can eat it as is, but it develops its flavour in the fridge, so it won't be as good.
  9. Enjoy!
