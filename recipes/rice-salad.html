Rice Salad

Description

A favourite Italian summer recipe of mine. This rice salad or "Insalata di riso", like the italians call it, is in every italians fridge all summer long.

It is a great recipe, when you don't know what to eat, because it mostly uses left over ingredients, which everybody has lying around.

Ingredients

  • 500g of Cooked rice (best if its leftover from the fridge)
  • 100g of Corn
  • 100g of Cherry tomatoes
  • Salt and Black pepper
  • 100g of Ham
  • 50g of Olives
  • 100g of any type of hard Cheese
  • 2 tablespoons of Olive oil
  • Some basil

Steps

  1. Cut the tomatoes, the ham and the cheese, into similarly sized cubes.
  2. Into a large bowl, mix all of the ingredients (rice, corn, tomatoes, ham, olives and cheese).
  3. Add the olive oil, basil leaves, and salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Leave the salad, for a few hours in the fridge its flavours can develop. Alternatively, consume straight away after making.
  5. Enjoy.
