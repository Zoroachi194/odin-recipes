Pesto Pasta

Description

This is one of my favourite recipes, because it is so easy and quick to make. It takes very little ingredients and you can use any pasta shape you want.

Pesto pasta is one of nord Italy's staples, just like pasta bolognese or carbonara. This is why it is an important recipe to learn, if you deem yourself as someone who knows Italian cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 1 package of Pasta (its shape doesn't matter)
  • 250g Pesto genovese
  • 100g Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • A few cherry tomatoes
  • Some basil

Steps

  1. Fill a pot with water and bring to a boil. After it starts boiling, add a genorous pinch of salt and the pasta. After around 10 minutes the pasta is ready and needs to be strained. Keep half a cup of the pasta water.
  2. In a large pan, add the pesto, the ground pepper, and salt to taste. After that we can add the pasta, pasta water, and the parmesan. Mix for around 2 minutes, until all the pasta is coated with a thick pesto sauce and the cheese has melted.
  3. Cut your cherry tomatoes and break the basil leaves using your hands to preserve flavour.
  4. Serve each dish with a few cherry tomatoes, some basil pieces and some more parmesan cheese to taste.
  5. Enjoy!
